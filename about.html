Disclosure

An AI coding assistant was used while building this site, for structural work: writing and refactoring code, managing files, and checking that what shipped ran.

The music, cover art, logos and photographs are nabii's own and were made without it. The dreams were written by people. No part of the artwork or writing on this site is machine-generated.

What this site stores

A dream is stored as its text, the name it was signed with, and the time it arrived. There are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. A dream cannot be traced back to the person who left it, including by us.

An email address, if given, is stored separately and used only to announce a release or a show. It is never published, sold or shared. To be removed from the list, ask.
