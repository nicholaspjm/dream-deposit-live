Disclosure

An AI coding assistant was used while building this site. It did the structural work: writing and refactoring code, moving files around, wiring things together, and checking that what shipped actually ran.

The music, the cover art, the logos and the photographs are nabii's own and were made without it. The dreams on the wall were written by people. Nothing here is machine-generated art or machine-written lyrics, and the design direction was decided by hand, with the assistant doing the typing.

What this site keeps

A dream is stored as its text, whatever name you signed it with, and the time it arrived. Nothing else. There are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics, and a dream can't be traced back to whoever left it, including by us.

An email address, if you give one, is stored on its own and used only to tell you about a release or a show. It is never published, never sold, and never handed to anyone else. Ask and it comes off the list.
